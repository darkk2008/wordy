Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

wwordy ("we", "us") is a vocabulary-learning app. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have. We try to collect as little as possible.

1. What stays on your device

Your word stacks, progress, settings and game results are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. If you never create an account, this data is not sent to us.

2. Account and sync (optional)

If you create an account to sync between devices, we store:

  • the ID you choose (a username, not necessarily your real name), and an internal technical identifier;
  • your password, which is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and stored only in hashed form;
  • a copy of your app data (stacks, words, progress and settings) so it can be restored on your other devices.

We do not ask for your email address or phone number. Because there is no email on the account, we cannot reset a forgotten password for you, so please keep it safe. You can delete your account and its stored data at any time from Settings > Account > Delete account.

3. AI features

When you use AI features (such as the AI tutor, translations, or filling in word forms), the text you send, for example the words and your question, is sent through our server to an AI provider (Groq) to generate the answer. Do not enter personal or confidential information into these features. We do not use this text to build a profile of you. If you add your own API key in the app, requests go directly from your device to the provider under their terms.

4. Usage statistics (off by default)

Anonymous usage statistics (for example which screens are opened) are collected with PostHog only if you turn them on in Settings > Privacy. You can turn them off again at any time.

5. Technical data

  • Device ID: a random identifier generated on your device so we can count distinct devices and make sure promo codes are used as intended. It is not linked to your name.
  • Online presence: while the app is open, it sends a random per-tab identifier so we can show how many people are online. It is deleted when you leave or after about a minute.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider (Netlify) processes standard request data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Promo codes: when you redeem a code, the code and your device ID are recorded.

7. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising cookies. The app uses your browser's local storage and a service worker (for offline use) to save your data and settings. Clearing your browser data removes them.

8. How long we keep data

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Presence identifiers expire within about a minute. If you want data removed and cannot use the in-app delete button, contact us.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict processing. You can export your data from Settings > Data, delete your account in the app, or contact us for anything else.

10. Children

wwordy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Connections use HTTPS and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: message us on Telegram at @calledww.
