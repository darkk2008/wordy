How it works

  1. Pick your language. Choose what you are learning and the language you already speak.
  2. Add words. Type them in, or ask the built-in AI tutor to build a themed stack for you.
  3. Play. Run a game or a quick flashcard round. The app tracks what you know.
  4. Come back tomorrow. Review the words that are due. A few minutes a day beats long sessions.

Questions

Is wwordy free?

Yes. You can build stacks, use flashcards and several games for free. Premium unlocks more games, more AI features and higher limits.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is saved on your device. An account is only needed if you want your stacks synced across devices.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once installed. AI features, such as filling word forms, need an internet connection.

How do I move my words to another device?

Sign in with the same account on both devices, or open Settings and use Sync via QR to send a stack straight to another phone.

What happens to my data?

Read the Privacy Policy. The short version: your words stay on your device unless you sign in to sync, and usage statistics are off unless you turn them on.
